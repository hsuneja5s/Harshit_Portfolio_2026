Research Copilot

A PwC engagement plugin that generates editable information architectures, persona cards, user journey maps, and wireframe starters on canvas directly from raw PRDs.

Overview

From a raw PRD to a canvas full of editable research artifacts.

A PwC engagement plugin. Drop in a raw Product Requirement Document and it builds editable information architecture, personas, journey maps, and starter wireframes right on the canvas, all pulled from the source document.

Problem

The translation loop between documents and design ate the start of every project.

Turning a dense requirements doc into IA, personas, and flows was manual, slow, and easy to get subtly wrong. Gaps and contradictions in the brief often surfaced only in the review meeting, when they were expensive to fix.

Outcome

Research scaffolding in minutes, gaps caught before the review.

Teams start from editable drafts instead of a blank canvas. A built in Devil's Advocate mode points out the gaps and contradictions right there, so missing requirements show up before the big review, not in the middle of it.
