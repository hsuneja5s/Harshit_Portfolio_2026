Designing for return visits, not test bookings

This isn't a booking app with reports attached. It's a daily companion for living with a chronic condition.

India's first accessibility-first diagnostics app, reframing transactional lab reports into longitudinal trend charts and plain-language indicators for elderly patients.

RoleSenior Product Designer
TeamPwC India

In brief

01 / CHALLENGE

Stale PDFs

Chronic patients booked tests once, received cryptic PDFs they couldn't interpret, and failed to return next quarter.

02 / THE BET

Companion Trends

Pivot the core flow from transaction bookings to a daily trend visualizer, designing exclusively around senior accessibility.

03 / RESULT

India's 1st

First fully accessible diagnostics app supporting voice bookings, clear sparkline histories, and fast report returns.

The behavior-change problem

Static, jargon-heavy PDF reports made it hard for chronic patients to track health metrics across quarters.

"I got the PDF. I didn't understand the number. I showed it to my son — he didn't know either. We just waited for the next doctor visit."

— Asha, 64 · Type 2 diabetic · user research session

A companion bet

We placed clinical metrics behind a plain-language translation layer, giving patients immediate visual status readings.

Trend-first companion

The interface transforms dry numerical worksheets into intuitive visual sparklines, voice booking prompts, and natural reading templates.

01 / Dashboard Pivot: Replacing cryptic medical jargon with simplified progress maps.
02 / Sparkline History: Multi-quarter sparkline tracks for quick trend reviews.
03 / Natural Reading Card: Plain-language summaries replacing complex biochemical tables.

Accessibility floor

Instead of treating accessibility as a checklist item, we built Apple HIG and WCAG standards directly into the type scaling, touch zones, and voice-assisted flows.

  • Touch Stability: Standard 48pt tap targets to accommodate tremulous hands.
  • Visual Clarity: AAA-compliant high-contrast themes optimized for outdoor glare.
  • No Motion-As-Data: Trend indicators relying purely on shape, labels, and color.

What we didn't ship

  • Multi-profile accounts. Deferred to ensure a bulletproof single-patient console.
  • Doctor PDF shortcuts. Cut in favor of simplified on-screen sharing cards.
  • Pharmacy integrations. Deliberately blocked to protect absolute patient data privacy.
