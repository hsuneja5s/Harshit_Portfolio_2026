Designing AI Assisted Experiences for Preventive Healthcare

Designed a consumer diagnostic experience helping users discover relevant tests, understand reports, and track long-term health patterns using contextual AI.

RoleProduct Designer
IndustryHealthcare Diagnostics
Duration6 Months
TeamProduct, Engineering, Business
FocusAI UX, Consumer Experience, Health Insights

The Challenge & Insights

Diagnostic apps often swamp users with overwhelming catalogs and dense worksheets, prompting anxiety rather than guidance. We designed a calm, profile-aware assistant to guide patient understanding rather than automate medical decisions.

Core friction points:

  • Context: Pinpointing relevant tests within dense listings.
  • Interpretation: Deciphering complex numbers prior to doctor visits.
  • Trust: Navigating promotional upsells instead of clinical advice.
Context & Relevance

"Tell me why this test matters to me, not just a list."

Interpretation

"I want simple understanding before visiting a doctor."

Proactive Trends

"Help me notice long-term trends earlier, rather than single data points."

Design Goal

Position AI as a thoughtful guide rather than automation—building confidence, agency, and clarity in personal health journeys.

Explain
Make health data simple and highly contextual.
Calm
Mitigate patient anxiety with neutral clinical framing.
Personalize
Tailor information to age and individual history.
Control
Put the user in absolute charge of their detail depth.

Key Experiences

We structured the AI assistance into three touchpoints that guide users quietly through discovery, interpretation, and explanation.

Confidence, Not Compliance Explain, Don't Persuade

Choosing the Right Tests

A symptom-aware recommendation layer that suggests contextually relevant packages, explaining exactly why they matter.

Key Decisions

  • Symptom-first: Describe feelings rather than test names.
  • Context: Adapt to profile age, past results, and history.
  • Rationale: Explain the exact physiological relevance.
Contextual recommendations with clear rationale.
Clarity Over Intelligence

Understandable Reports

A redesigned Diagnostics Summary leading with a calm natural-language status card, followed by multi-quarter trends.

Key Decisions

  • Status-first: Summarize outcomes calmly at the very top.
  • Sparklines: Showcase longitudinal historical shifts.
  • Disclosure: Keep detailed clinical worksheets on-demand.
Historical sparkline trends and natural language interpretation.
Emotional Sensitivity Explain, Don't Persuade

Explainability & Trust

An explainability layer that traces all AI-generated suggestions back to personal history and concrete clinical rules.

Explainability Framework

  • Traceability: Highlight the exact signal that prompted advice.
  • Context: Explain how it ties to individual medical profiles.
  • Transparency: Provide clear source paths over persuasive text.
Traceable and context-aware medical explanations.

Reflection

In healthcare, the most effective AI intervention is the one that reduces noise, not the one that adds features. By reframing the assistant from an "intelligent agent" to a "contextual guide," we built consumer agency rather than dependence—ensuring patients leave the experience informed and confident rather than anxious.
