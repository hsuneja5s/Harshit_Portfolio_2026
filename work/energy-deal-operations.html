The story

The finance function at a global energy enterprise oversees a highly complex portfolio of regional deals, but critical project records, resource capacity, and budgets lived in separate worksheets and disconnected software suites.

Answering fundamental questions—which opportunities are currently compliance-ready, where budgets stand, and whether capacity is constrained—demanded hours of manual data assembly, forcing month-end reviews to depend entirely on offline spreadsheets and slide decks.

The bet

We reframed the problem from "another dashboard" to a centralized landing cockpit called **The Pacific Platform**. We made one explicit call: **bring active deals, resources, and budget utilization together in a tabbed, high-density interface.**

Instead of building heavy analytical scenario builders, we focused on legibility—putting the key metadata (Decision Executive, IM Advisor, Country) and opportunity status checklists directly on the deal card, so review teams could confirm compliance in one scan.

The cockpit

The dashboard cockpit organizes the highly complex deal pipeline into four workflow-aligned views, replacing fragmented regional files with a single interactive, high-density system.

01 / Budget Cockpit: Live actual vs. planned cost tracking with active risk alerts directly in the dashboard interface.
02 / Resource Contributors: Dedicated mapping of project coordinators, Opportunity Executives, and assurance reviewers in one unified list.
03 / Opportunity Details: Structured high-density metadata detail tabs covering LNG strategic themes, country flags, and confidentiality tiers.
04 / Stage Gate Checks: Compliance checklists tracking SharePoint workspace provisioning and WBS setups to enforce governance.

Insight.AI

The Budget tab embeds **Insight.AI**, an active risk layer designed to flag spend trajectory anomalies proactively where budget reviews occur.

  • Contextual AI Warnings: Restrained, high-visibility warning blocks alerting teams directly: "Burn rate has increased by 18% this month. At this pace, budget exhaustion is projected 3 weeks before closure."
  • Granular Category breakdown: Maps costs by category (FM Staff actual hours and costs) next to a clean monthly spend trend bar chart.
  • Longitudinal spend trends: Displays multi-year trajectories at a glance, allowing immediate context checks against historical averages.

What we didn't ship

Every deliberate cut bought the cockpit a better chance of actually being used.

  • Complex scenario modeling. We avoided adding heavy simulation tools in v1 to prioritize platform legibility. Establishing a single source of truth for active deals took precedence.
  • Granular custom report builders. Held the line on a standardized, shared vocabulary. Custom review views were deferred to v2 to prevent fragmentation of review standards.
  • Full contributor writing permissions. The initial release prioritized the leader-out review cockpit, keeping data entry fields highly focused and lightweight.

With structured deal data and workflows in one place, the platform serves as a solid foundation for future integrations, including trend projections across quarters and expanded AI-driven indicators of deal health.
