Deal intelligence for global energy operations

A unified control hub The Pacific Platform that consolidated multiple separate trackers and hundreds of active regional deals into a single, workflow aligned interface, shifting finance from manual reconciliation to active performance management.

RoleSenior Product Designer
ClientGlobal energy enterprise
TimelineJun 2025 Dec 2025

The challenge

Stitch together fragmented active deal data, time bookings, and budgets scattered across separate regional teams into a single workflow cockpit.

In brief

01 / CONSOLIDATE

Spreadsheet Chaos

Gather regional time bookings and live opportunity budgets into a singular pipeline.

Unified Cockpit

03 / THE SHIFT

In Flight Checks

The story

Regional energy deals lived in separate spreadsheets, making status and budget tracking hours of manual work.

Deal and budget cockpit.

The bet

We bet on clean, high density legibility putting key opportunity metadata and compliance checklists directly on the primary deal cards.

The cockpit

The dashboard cockpit organizes the highly complex deal pipeline into five workflow aligned views.

01 / Budget Cockpit: Live actual vs. planned cost tracking with burn rate alerts.
02 / Detailed View: Core opportunity details and metadata.
03 / Historic Dashboard: Historical margins and status compliance pipelines.
04 / Activity Log: Audit trails tracking gate clearances.
05 / Portfolio Manager View: Executive dashboard with burn and compliance trends.

Insight.AI

A conversational assistant built directly into the platform to guide finance leaders through deal performance and predictions.

  • Conversational Guidance: Chat prompts let leaders query burn rates, budgets, and forecasts in plain language.
  • Proactive Risk Checks: Flags anomalies and queries security metrics before they become critical.
Conversational AI assistant interface.

What we didn't ship

  • Complex scenario modeling. Excluded to focus phase-1 on a single source of truth.
  • Custom report builders. Standardized review taxonomy to keep dashboards unified.
  • Full editing permissions. Capped inputs in v1 to keep data entry lightweight.
