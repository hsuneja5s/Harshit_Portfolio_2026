AI banking assistant — turning AI hesitation into self-service

Conversation as a medium is fine. Conversation as a prerequisite is the trap.

A bank's first AI assistant designed to convert hesitant customers into successful self-service users, resolving cold starts through quick-action chips and structured interactive cards.

RoleSenior Product Designer
TeamPwC India
TimelineMar 2024 — Dec 2024

In brief

01 / CHALLENGE

Cold-Start Freeze

~70% of users froze when shown a blank chat input, turning potential self-service opportunities into support tickets.

02 / THE BET

Chips Over Chat

Open with 8 high-frequency action chips. Enable free-text routing only once the conversation is successfully in flight.

03 / RESULT

NPS 4.7 → 9.2

Drastically improved task completion and deflected 30%+ of routine customer service volumes to digital channels.

The activation problem

Showing users an empty text box led to massive cognitive friction. Instead of conversing, users typed ambiguous, single-word queries, failed to get answers, and abandoned the flow.

"I don't know what to ask."

— banking customer, usability testing

The chips bet

We bypassed the blank-screen obstacle by providing a visible menu of the **eight most-asked support tasks**. This defined clear boundaries of capability in a single scan.

Conversational card cockpit

Instead of responding with long blocks of assistant text, the interface resolves user prompts directly into structured, interactive transaction and payment cards.

01 / Action Menu: High-density quick chips bypassing the chat freeze by prompting frequent commands.
02 / Interactive Result Card: Live transaction ledger mapping Amazon, fuel, and deposits securely on-canvas.
03 / Structured Task Card: Multi-step contextual wizard helping users transfer money in-context.

Refusal matrix

In regulated retail banking, establishing trust depends entirely on mapping exactly what the assistant **won't** do. We built safety limits directly into the product core.

  • Regulatory Guardrails: Automatic refusal to comment on markets, mutual funds, or provide financial advice.
  • Non-Binding Intent: Requires explicit checkbox approval before any cash-out or transaction executes.
  • Graceful Escalate: Transparently shifts the user context to live chat and active support within seconds of fraud flagging.
Safe AI fallback interface.

What we didn't ship

Every deliberate cut bought us launch velocity and system legibility.

  • Extended 12-chip panel. Deferred to keep the initial dashboard interface completely visible above the fold, preventing pagination friction.
  • Guided legacy tours. Cut in favor of native-state contextual prompts, decreasing initial sprint overhead.
  • Chatbot error recovery dialogs. Cut to prioritize immediate support handoff over building complex error-correction scripts.
