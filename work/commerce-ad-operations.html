B2B self-serve ad dashboard.

From approval queues to self-serve campaign management

The design problem wasn't "give the brand manager a nicer interface." It was "unbundle the concierge so the platform can monetise its full inventory."

An operational unbundling of an account-management bottleneck inside a commerce marketplace, transforming slow, email-driven concierge queues into an intuitive, self-serve ads console.

RoleSenior Product Designer
TeamCommerce marketplace
Timeline2022 — 2023

In brief

01 / CHALLENGE

Concierge Cap

Every ad campaign required a platform representative, setting a severe ceiling on inventory sales and brand seat scaling.

02 / THE BET

Designed for Priya

Build a 3-tab layout optimized for brand managers who spend 20% of their week inside our app, rather than daily power-users.

03 / RESULT

Unlocked Inventory

Fast campaign launches without platform reps in the loop. Direct drop in support tickets and strategic AdOps refocus.

A revenue-ceiling problem

Manual concierge queues bottlenecked ad campaign scaling, capping marketplace inventory monetisation.

"What's happening with my ad?"

— Most-asked question in brand manager usability testing

Operational unbundling

We unified the platform into exactly three tabs, bypassing complex multi-campaign analytical widgets to accelerate adoption.

The campaign cockpit

The portal guides brand managers through secure creative uploads, placement select grids, and real-time status updates.

01 / Launch Wizard: Guided three-step progressive campaign configuration.
02 / Status Tracker: High-density state cue cards showing review stages.
03 / B2B Funnel: Direct conversion funnel tracking click-through rates.

Placement hierarchy

Instead of building generic targeting selectors, the cockpit links campaigns directly to live marketplace sections and native bidding layouts.

  • Unified Placements: Structured selectors targeting prime ad locations.
  • Creative Review Logs: Status indicators paired with moderator comment cards.
  • 6-Step Funnel Metrics: ROI mapping showing clicks and impressions.

Inventory

Strategic

What we didn't ship

  • First-time tours. Omitted in favor of intuitive layout primitives.
  • Cross-brand analytics. Deferred to future releases to protect sprint focus.
  • Drag-and-drop builders. Scoped out to guarantee asset formatting compliance.
