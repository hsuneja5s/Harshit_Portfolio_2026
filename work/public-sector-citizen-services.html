Designing citizen trust into a national platform

Every design decision is a trust deposit or a trust withdrawal.

A unified citizen-services interface designed to connect citizens, officers, and state machinery across India, scaling access from metro lawyers to rural farmers on low-bandwidth devices.

In brief

01 / CHALLENGE

Institutional Trust

Establish a highly legible and accessible digital interface that citizens trust over local physical stations.

02 / THE BET

Single Surface

Refuse customized state surfaces in favor of one unified layout, containing backend-driven state variance.

03 / RESULT

Frictionless Scale

Streamlined complaint routing and real-time status tracking for 1.4B+ citizens across all states and UTs.

The trust problem

Citizens only adopt digital filing if they trust it over physical station visits, requiring high visual legitimacy.

Complaint status & verification timeline.

The strategic call

We unified the interface into a single national layout, handling custom regional variance dynamically in the backend.

Filing & status cockpit

Citizen filing flows and officer review cues are organized into high-density dashboard layouts.

01 / Filing Form: Supports local inputs, voice-first paths, and smart categories.
02 / Officer Dashboard: Console for evidence management and verification queues.

Federated scale

The platform adapts automatically to regional dialects, literacy levels, and low bandwidth constraints.

  • Localization: Clean translations with voice assistance.
  • Offline Fallbacks: State sync ensuring form safety on slow networks.
Offline-first mobile interface.

Where it earned its keep

↑ Adoption
Daily traffic shifted from physical queues to the unified digital cockpit.
↓ Resolution
Attributable drop in case resolution timelines via standardized routing.
28 + 8
States and UTs unified under a single, non-forked citizen vocabulary.

What we didn't ship

  • Real-time citizen-officer chat. Excluded to avoid archiving complex unstructured records.
  • State-customizable branding. Blocked to maintain a singular, trustworthy national brand language.
  • Voice-only application flows. Scoped out to prioritize multi-language keyboard entries.
