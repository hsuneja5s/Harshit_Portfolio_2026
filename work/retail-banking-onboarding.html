The economics of fewer decisions

Every removed decision is a recovered customer, a recovered application, a recovered revenue line.

A UX overhaul of high-stakes banking flows — loan applications, cross-currency transfers, and account opening — reframed purely as a decision-reduction problem.

RoleSenior Product Designer
TeamPwC India
TimelineJun 2024 — Nov 2024

In brief

01 / CHALLENGE

Friction & Abandonment

High drop-out rates in high-revenue flows due to excessive cognitive and decision load (e.g. 14 decisions in loan application).

02 / THE BET

Design Decisions

Redesign flow logic to demand the absolute minimum decisions. Shift the workload from customer typing to confirmation chips.

03 / RESULT

14 Decisions to 6

Drastically shortened paths, collapsed pending-action drift, and unified B2C elements on ~85% library reuse.

The abandonment problem

Redundant KYC requirements and manual entries drove high abandonment in high-revenue banking flows.

"I started the loan form three times. Each time I got to a page that asked me things I didn't have with me and I gave up."

— private-sector bank customer, exit research interview

Decision reduction bet

We focused entirely on progressive disclosure, consolidating input steps into a single active flow container.

Decision reduction cockpit

By restructuring user steps, the platform replaces manual typing with context-aware smart widgets and real-time calculators.

01 / Legacy Maze: Audit of the highly repetitive 14-decision legacy onboarding.
02 / Redesigned Path: Unified 6-decision dashboard with prefilled transaction parameters.
03 / Attention Primitive: Visual cue pointing users exactly to where their action is stalled.

Prefills & smart defaults

Smart defaults and reusable tokens bypass manual entry, giving the customer actionable confirmation chips instead of intimidating blank fields.

  • Prefilled KYC: Dynamic PAN and registration mapping.
  • Smart Defaults: Parameter presets based on historical volume.
  • Library Reuse: Semantic design tokens driving ~85% layout reuse.
KYC prefill confirmation chips.

What we didn't ship

  • Clean data layers. Scoped out for release to prioritize frontend token mapping fallbacks.
  • Contribution dashboards. Deferred in favor of standardized figma files.
  • Vertical governance docs. Trimmed into a single framework to save sprint overhead.
